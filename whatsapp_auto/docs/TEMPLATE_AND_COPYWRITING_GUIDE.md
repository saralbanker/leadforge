# WhatsApp Copywriting & Template Guide for Ahmedabad B2B Industrialists

## 1. The WhatsApp Psychology of Gujarat Factory Owners
When an industrialist in Vatva, Odhav, Changodar, or Kathwada opens WhatsApp:
- They are usually walking the shop floor, checking raw material prices, or reviewing transport dispatch photos.
- They have zero tolerance for corporate jargon, long marketing spiels, or artificial pleasantries.
- They evaluate every incoming cold message in **under 3 seconds**.

### Why "Hi Team" Fails Completely on WhatsApp
- WhatsApp is an intimate, direct 1-to-1 personal channel.
- Opening with *"Hi team"* immediately signals a generic, unresearched bulk mail-merge blast.
- It insults the factory owner who is reading the message on their private phone.

## 2. Proven WhatsApp Templates (Ahmedabad Industrial Focus)

### Template A (Primary): WhatsApp-to-Tally Order Entry Hook
**Angle**: Eliminating manual data re-entry between WhatsApp customer orders and Tally accounting.

```text
Hello Sir, noticed {company_name} manufactures {products} in {area}.

When repeat orders come in on WhatsApp or phone, does your staff have to re-type those items into Tally by hand?

We built a simple tool for Ahmedabad manufacturers that pushes WhatsApp orders directly into Tally without re-typing.

Can I share a 30-second demo video here?

Saral Banker
Orvion, Ahmedabad
```

#### Why Template A Works:
1. **Direct Salutation**: *"Hello Sir,"* is respectful, culturally natural, and professional across Gujarat manufacturing units.
2. **Immediate Local Anchor**: Mentions their exact product and GIDC estate (e.g. *valves in Odhav*, *chemical dyes in Vatva*).
3. **Paints an Everyday Pain Point**: 90% of Gujarat MSME orders arrive as WhatsApp photos or text messages that an office clerk spends 2 hours every evening re-typing into Tally.
4. **Basic, Clear Vocabulary**: Uses plain words (*"simple tool"*, *"re-type items"*, *"pushes orders"*) rather than tech jargon (*"AI-powered ERP synchronization middleware"*).
5. **Ultra Low-Friction Call-to-Action (CTA)**: *"Can I share a 30-second demo video here?"* The prospect doesn't have to schedule a call or leave WhatsApp. They just reply *"Yes"* or *"Share"*.

## 3. Strict Rules & Guardrails

| Do This ✅ | Never Do This ❌ |
| :--- | :--- |
| Use *"Hello Sir,"* or *"Hello,"* | Never use *"Hi team"*, *"Dear Sir/Madam"*, or *"Hey buddy"* |
| Mention their specific GIDC area (Vatva, Odhav, Kathwada, Sanand, Changodar) | Never use generic geography like *"In your region"* |
| Ask to send a 30-second video or 1-page PDF directly on WhatsApp | Never ask for a *"15-minute Zoom discovery call"* on Touch 1 |
| Mention your local presence (*"Orvion, Ellisbridge/Ahmedabad"*) | Never pretend to be a customer asking for quotes |
| Keep the entire message under **50–65 words** | Never send walls of text or unformatted paragraphs |
| Use basic English or simple Gujarati phrasing if appropriate | Never use buzzwords: *"synergy"*, *"cutting-edge"*, *"AI-driven"*, *"disrupt"* |
